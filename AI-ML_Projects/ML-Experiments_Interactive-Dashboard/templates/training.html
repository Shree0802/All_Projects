Laboratory Methodology

Zero Data Leakage: All data transformations and scalers are fitted exclusively on X_train and evaluated on X_test.

Stratified Partitioning: Classification datasets utilize stratified sampling to preserve proportional class distributions.

Persistent Artifact Safety: Newly trained models are saved to models/retrained/ with timestamps, protecting core lab models.
